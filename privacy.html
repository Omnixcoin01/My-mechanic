Privacy Policy

My Mechanic

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Thank you for using My Mechanic. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number
  • Your current location (only when requesting a mechanic)
  • Basic device information needed for the app to work properly

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send a mechanic to your location.
  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To respond to your service requests.
  • To improve the quality of our services.
  • To provide customer support.

3. Location Permission

Location access is requested only when you ask for a mechanic. We do not continuously track your location.

4. Data Security

We use Google Firebase to securely store your information. We take reasonable measures to keep your personal information safe and protected.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your information is shared only with the mechanic handling your request when necessary to provide the requested service.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where permitted by law.

7. Children's Privacy

My Mechanic is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
